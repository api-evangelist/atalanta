# Atalanta

> Atalanta is a mathematical AI company building provably correct decision-making for the world's most important missions. Its flagship platform, Argo, uses mathematical proof and formal verification to establish safety, security, and performance guarantees for software-defined and autonomous systems at operational speed. Atalanta was founded by Anjana Rajan and Jonathan Ring, is headquartered in Washington, D.C., and is backed by Felicis and In-Q-Tel.
